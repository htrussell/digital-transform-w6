2 · Accountable rationale

Why each person has the final say.

Decisions marked "Due this quarter" are the ones listed on the QBR dashboard.

DecisionAccountableWhy this role (2–3 sentences)
D1. Automation budget allocation
Budget allocation		 EVP & CFO C1, C2, C3 and R1 compete for one capital pool for supply chain and technology, which grew from $11.8B in FY2024 to $14.6B in FY2025 [E2][E3]. No operating leader should decide funding for their own project, so the CFO, who owns capital allocation across Walmart, has the final say, with the U.S. CEO consulted on priorities.
D2. Shared automation platform standards
Architecture standards		 EVP, Global CTO & Chief Development Officer Automation runs across 164 U.S. distribution facilities [E3], and they only work as one network if their systems stay compatible. Technology also changes hands: Walmart bought Alphabot's developer in 2022 [E1] and sold that business to Symbotic in 2025 [G4][G5]. The enterprise technology leader approves exceptions, not any single facility program.
D3. Demand, inventory and delivery data ownership
Data governance		 EVP & COO, Walmart U.S. The COO is responsible for all U.S. store operations and supply chain [G1], which covers both places this data is used: AI inventory positioning (W1) and delivery zones (R2). Walmart lists no Chief Data Officer among its executive officers [E3], so the operating owner of the data holds the decision.
D4. Which network gets automated next
Initiative prioritizationDue this quarter		 EVP & COO, Walmart U.S. With limited capacity, Walmart must choose between more parcel stations (W2) [S5] and grocery warehouse automation (R1) [S7]. This is a trade-off between delivery and warehouse capacity, and the COO is the only role over both [G1], so neither side decides for the other.
D5. Automation and robotics partners
Vendor & partnership		 EVP, Supply Chain, Walmart U.S. Partner equipment, from Symbotic's systems in regional DCs [G4] to Fox Robotics' forklifts [S6], runs inside the warehouses the supply chain team operates, so that leader owns the relationships. The CTO checks technical fit, and the CFO is consulted because Walmart took an ownership stake in Fox Robotics [S6].
D6. Pilot-to-scale gate
Case-specificDue this quarter		 EVP, President & CEO, Walmart U.S. Scaling a test commits money, facilities and workers across supply chain, eCommerce and stores at once, so it needs the leader over all of Walmart U.S. Walmart's own deals work this way: more forklifts come only with continued performance [S6][G3], and 400 Symbotic store systems only if targets are met [G4].
D7. Associate role transition
Case-specificDue this quarter		 EVP & Chief People Officer, Walmart U.S. This role oversees people efforts for all Walmart U.S. associates in stores, distribution centers and fulfillment centers [G2], the workforce every automation wave affects. Walmart trains associates to run the new forklifts [S6], and R1 requires retraining workers into technology-enabled roles.
Gaps we acknowledge
  • The R, C and I placements and each Accountable choice are our team's analysis of Walmart's published structure, not decision rights Walmart has disclosed.
  • The matrix names roles, not people; several of these roles changed hands between 2022 and 2025.
